About

A secure home for the tools you build.

derhead.app exists because custom MCP servers shouldn't live on localhost forever — and they shouldn't require you to compromise on security to get them into ChatGPT Business.

We're building the hosting layer: one trusted place to deploy, protect, and manage every MCP server and app you ship from Cursor.

"Build the tool in Cursor. Host it on derhead. Your team uses it in ChatGPT Business — without exposing your stack to the open internet."

— The derhead.app vision
